e-Campus General 

Unable to access Timecard Field

  • If a staff cannot access their Timecards, then they need to contact the person at their job that hired them. The person that processed their hiring paperwork didn't process them properly.

  • If a student cannot access their Timecards, then they need to contact their hiring manager or department. The hiring manager didn't process them properly.

Faculty Access to Student Records

For any inquiries regarding faculty access (or any other faculty/staff access) to student records in e-Campus, refer the users to submit this form:  https://web.uri.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/1376/PS-Financials-Request-for-Access-Form_New-May2022.pdf to this email: essars@etal.uri.edu and 401-874-2522 (Fax: 401-874-2910).

Changing e-Campus Username & Email Address

  • For students: Enrollment Services has the right to reject any request for changing the URI e-Campus Username and Email Address IF IT DOES NOT meet the following requirement: the current username is offensive/inappropriate, the student is transitioning gender or the username contains a portion of the student’s Social Security Number. For more information: https://web.uri.edu/registration-records/for-students/change-e-campus-username-email-address/.

  • For faculty and staff: they must contact URI's Human Resources Administration (HR): https://web.uri.edu/hr/ 

  • eCampus and Brightspace data syncs 1-2 times every day

Error Code: "Not a private connection" 

 If people are having troubles getting to its.uri.edu/e-Campus (saying it is not a private connection) it is usually because there are multiple redirects surrounding that website, which makes it look suspicious to web browsers. To avoid this have them go to the direct log-in page:

Student Portal - ecampus.uri.edu/student

Faculty/Staff - ecampus.uri.edu/faculty-staff

(Both are just regular e-Campus, so credentials should work the same on each)

Error Code: "SORRY! The information you provided does NOT MATCH what we have on record. Please verify what has been entered."

The error message appears when the record on e-Campus does not match with the information the user is entering. 

If the first name is hyphenated in e-Campus, then enter it as one entire name without the hyphen (Ami-Nah in e-Campus should be entered as Aminah)

If the last name is hyphenated in e-Campus, enter it as one entire name without the hyphen (Ami-Nah in e-Campus should be entered as Aminah) OR enter one or the other hyphenated name (Ami or Nah)

If the first and last name is not hyphenated, and the URI ID number is being entered correctly, ask the user to contact (401) 874-4530

Error Code: "YOU ARE NOT AUTHORIZED TO VIEW THIS PAGE"

This error means that you're signed into e-campus somewhere on a browser, meaning it is a remembered sign in. (use incognito window)

Make sure they are not in a work or school network as they might restrict certain e-campus websites including the forget user ID website. This is because the port number for the e-campus log in page is in the range that is typically blocked by work networks. Also the redirection of the webpage from uri.edu to appprod.uri.edu results in the network blocking the site

e-Campus Student

Initial Enrollment & e-Bill Payments

  • New URI students will need to pay an "initial enrollment" deposit through Flywire which they will have to create an account for: uri.myonplanu.com/login. Non-Matriculating and returning students are an exception to this.  Non-Matriculating students will need to get in touch with Enrollment Services to ensure their paperwork has went through, with Returning Students needing to check in with Admissions.

  • For new students, they will need to get into their e-Campus account and go to: e-Campus --> URI Student Services --> New Student Enrollment Deposit. From here, they can choose a plan with or without housing.

  • For students who need to pay URI bills, they need to log into their e-Campus account and go to: Financial Account --> My Payment and e-Bill Portal. After creating/logging into their Flywire Account will they be able to make payments towards any bills. Additionally, they can make payments as a guest too.

  • Any issues logging into Flywire accounts should have Enrollment Services contacted. Further information concerning viewing and making payments can be found here: https://web.uri.edu/tuition-billing/view-your-e-bill-and-make-payment/. They may also directly contact Flywire at 800-346-9252.

You do not have access to enroll - Non Matriculating/Non Degree Student

  • For non-matriculating (non-degree) students that are trying to enroll in classes, they must submit a non-degree online application for each semester they will be taking courses. These applications take 1 business day to process. If it's not done, the user will see the error message "You do not have access to enroll" when trying to register for classes. 

  • The non matriculating/non degree application can be found here: https://web.uri.edu/registration-records/forms/ 

Summer Registration Issues

When students are unable to enroll in any Summer courses:

  1. Ask if the student is a matriculating URI student. If the answer is no (as in the user is a non-matriculating student), then the student should continue to be referred to the URI Summer website and/or John Olerio for registration instructions.

  2. If the student is a matriculating URI student, it might mean that they submitted an intent to graduate form for May (rather than August) and this shut off their summer course registration. These students should be referred to their dean's office to request to have their intent to graduate pushed back from May to August.
    (August grads still participate in May commencement).

RhodyShare

Students may assign guest access to e-Campus to view their billing, academic records, and/or financial aid information. A guest can be their parent, guardian, grandparent, sibling, employer, or other third-party payers. https://web.uri.edu/registration-records/for-students/rhody-share-share-info-with-parent-guardian/.

  • If the username starts with URI_xxxxx, it's a clear indication that it's a RhodyShare account that the student had set up for guest access. The guest account CANNOT reset this password. The guest/parent would need to have their student reset their RhodyShare password.

  • Resetting RhodyShare password as a student: Once you enter your e-Campus account,  select the e-Campus Tab --> Click URI Student Services --> Select RhodyShare: Share Your Info --> Enter the xxxx part of URI_xxxx for the URI ID --> Insert new password twice --> Click Save.

  • NOTE: Certain changes on RhodyShare will need 24 hours in order to take effect.

Updating Off Campus Housing Information 

  1. Login to e-Campus and click the e-Campus tile.

  2. Select Self Service -> Campus Personal Information -> Addresses.

  3. Click Add a new address button, enter your address and click OK (even if your address is already listed on e-Campus).

  4. Select the Off Campus box and click Save.

There are various errors that can occur when registering for classes within e-Campus. The link provided goes over a majority of them, and what the cause is, as well as probable solutions too: https://web.uri.edu/registration-records/common-registration-error-messages/ .

Submitting Online Time Card

  1. Login to e-Campus > e-Campus tile > Self-Service on the left > Time Reporting > Timesheet

  2. Enter your hours per day by matching it with the hours reported on Aladtec/SubitUp

  3. Click URI Internal Regular Pay for the drop down menu under Time Reporting Code

  4. Click Submit to submit your hours. Hours should be submitted by the student every 2 weeks (bi-weekly). 

Accepting Graduate Matriculation

Graduate Students are required to accept matriculation in e-Campus.  After accepting it takes 1-2 buisness days for their accounts to be created.  They are not required to pay a deposit.

  • Select Self Service --> Student Admission --> Accept Admission

General Troubleshooting 

  • Clear cache, cookies, and browsing data.

  • Try a different browser.

  • Try incognito.

  • Make sure the e-Campus link isn't expired.

  • Check to see they're using a work network.

  • Make sure the user is connected to the VPN or have the appropriate software installed (For HR and Financials)

  • Reset their ecampus password and make sure they reset it again within e-campus to resync the passwords.

  • Make sure that the user is not using an old bookmarked link (links change after certain updates and old links will not work).
